Privacy Policy for Life Alarm

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · Contact: tech.bytxen01@gmail.com

Life Alarm (“the app”) is an alarm clock with optional sleep, water and medicine trackers. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The information you enter stays on your device. The developer does not receive it, sell it or share it. The only things that leave your device are listed in sections 3 and 4.

1. Information the app stores on your device

Everything below is created by you inside the app and is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone.

  • Alarms, timers, stopwatch and world clocks: times, labels, repeat days, chosen sounds, snooze and dismiss settings.
  • Profile (optional): your name, age, gender, weight, height, activity level, usual wake-up and bedtime, and sleep goal. It is used only to suggest a sleep schedule and a daily water target.
  • Water tracker (optional): your daily goal, reminder schedule and the drinks you log.
  • Sleep tracker (optional): your bedtime and wake-up schedule, and sleep sessions. While a night is being tracked, the app records when the screen turns on and off to estimate how well you slept. These events stay on the device and are deleted once the night has been summarised.
  • Medicine tracker (optional): medicine names, strengths, schedules, reminders, and whether you took, skipped or missed each dose.
  • Settings and preferences: theme, time format, and similar choices.

This information is health-related. The developer cannot see it. It is not used for advertising.

3. Information sent off your device

  1. Medicine name search. When you search for a medicine while adding it, the text you type is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to PubChem, a public chemistry database run by the U.S. National Institutes of Health (pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov). It returns the medicine’s name and chemical details. The app does not send your name, your schedule, your health profile or any account or device identifier with the request. Like any website, PubChem can see your IP address. Its handling of data is described in the NIH privacy notice. You can add a medicine by hand without using this search.
  2. Android backup. If Android’s backup is turned on for your phone, Android may back up the app’s data (alarms, settings, profile, medicine and tracker data) to your own Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. This is controlled by your phone’s backup setting and is handled by Google under Google’s privacy policy. The developer has no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone’s system settings.
  3. Smartwatch. If you use a paired Wear OS watch, Google Play services may exchange the next alarm’s time and label between the phone and the watch.
  4. Sounds from your files. If you pick your own alarm sound, the app remembers the location of that file. The audio file is not copied or sent anywhere.
  5. Google libraries. The app uses Google’s ML Kit (barcode scanning) and Google Play services (watch sync). These Google components may send limited technical diagnostics, such as performance data and device or app version information, to Google. This is covered by Google’s privacy policy. The developer does not receive it and it contains none of the information in section 1.

4. What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account or ask for your email address.
  • It does not contain advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • The developer has not added any analytics, tracking or crash-reporting service of its own.
  • It does not sell or share your information, and does not use it to profile you.
  • It does not read your contacts, messages, location, photos or microphone.

5. Medical disclaimer

The medicine and sleep features are reminders and logs. They are not medical advice and do not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Always follow your doctor’s or pharmacist’s instructions. Do not rely on the app as the only way to take medicine on time.

6. How long information is kept, and deleting it

Information stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual alarms, medicines and logs inside the app. To remove everything, clear the app’s storage in your phone’s settings or uninstall the app. Data already saved to your Android backup is managed in your Google account’s backup settings. Because the developer holds no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Security

The app’s data is kept in storage that other apps cannot read, and network requests use HTTPS. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so keep your phone locked and updated.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from children. The developer receives no personal information from any user.

9. Your rights

Because the developer does not collect or hold your personal information, there is no developer-held data to access, correct or delete. All of it is under your control on your device. If you have questions about privacy, email the contact address below.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of information changes, this policy will be updated and the effective date changed. Material changes will be explained in the app’s update notes.
